### Faculty

{{< director_member name="Jian Zhao" role="Assistant Professor" image="jian_zhao.png" academic="https://jianzhao-zgci.github.io" >}}
Assistant Professor and Ph.D. Advisor at Zhongguancun Academy, and Researcher at the Zhongguancun Institute of Artificial Intelligence. Received B.Eng. and Ph.D. degrees from the University of Science and Technology of China in 2018 and 2023, . The doctoral dissertation was nominated for the 2024 Outstanding Doctoral Dissertation Award in Agents and Multi-Agent Systems（only 5 nominees nationwide）.
Published over 40 papers in leading international conferences and journals, including NeurIPS, ICML, and ICLR. Winner of several academic competitions, including the inaugural Tencent Kaiwu Global Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning Competition and multiple RLChina Agent Challenges. Led industry collaboration projects with companies including Tencent and NetEase.
